How it works

  1. 1

    Sign out

    Enter your name, email, department, and contact number. Pick the items and a return date.

  2. 2

    Use

    An item you hold shows as out. Nobody else can sign it out until it comes back.

  3. 3

    Sign in

    Bring the item to the SHARE office and sign it in with the same email.

Past the return date, a reminder goes to your email at 8:00 AM every day until you sign the item in. It comes from share@spmcdvo.net.
